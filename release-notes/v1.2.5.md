# Chamfer Trackball v1.2.5

2026-10-28

- The shake is gone. In 3D modelling, tap the top-right button to make the ball pan, and tap it again to orbit. It never guesses wrong. In Normal mouse that button does nothing special.
- Rolling back and forth now simply orbits (it used to hold still, waiting for a shake).
- Switching to 3D modelling always starts in orbit.
