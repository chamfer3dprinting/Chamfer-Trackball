# Chamfer Trackball v1.2.3

2026-10-28

- Fixed 3D modelling acting on the wrong spot: before the ball moves the view, the pointer now jumps to the middle of the 3D app, so orbit, pan and circle-zoom always land on the model — never on a toolbar, a link or a browser tab (where the middle-button drag could open or close tabs).
- Normal orbiting in curves is no longer mistaken for zoom circles: zoom now needs a clear circle.
- Your settings are reset to the standard layout: Normal mouse and 3D modelling, left/right click normal, top-left swaps the two, top-right and wheels normal, default speeds. Macros, apps, theme and programs you made yourself are kept.
