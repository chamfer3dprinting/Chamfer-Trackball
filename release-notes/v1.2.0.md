# Chamfer Trackball v1.2.0

2026-10-28

- Ball-only 3D navigation, the new default: no keyboard and no holding buttons. The top-left key cycles four programs — Normal mouse → 3D orbit → 3D pan → 3D zoom — and in a 3D app the ball just orbits, pans or zooms. Outside 3D apps it's always a normal pointer.
- Default buttons: left and right click stay normal, top-left cycles programs, top-right does nothing (free for a macro), both wheels scroll normally.
- Each program has a new choice "In 3D apps, rolling the ball…": moves the pointer, orbits, pans or zooms.
- Your settings move over: the 3D modelling program's speeds and DPI go to the three 3D programs, Normal mouse keeps its own, and programs you made yourself and your macros stay.
