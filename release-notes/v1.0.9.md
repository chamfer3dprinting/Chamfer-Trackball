# Chamfer Trackball v1.0.9

2026-10-28

- The on-screen label now appears the moment you press an orbit, pan or zoom button in a 3D app ("Orbit — roll the ball"), then follows the direction you roll.
- Gestures per app: on Buttons & keys → Ball gestures, choose an app under "Gestures for" and give each direction a command just for that app. The same gesture can run Extrude in Fusion, Grab in Blender and something else in Chamfer3D Studio; anything not set uses the program's gesture.
- The trackball picture in the app shows what the ball is doing live: the ball lights up with an arrow for the direction you're rolling, + or − while zooming, and Orbiting, Panning or Zooming underneath. Home → Right now shows the direction too.
