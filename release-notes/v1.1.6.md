# Chamfer Trackball v1.1.6

2026-10-28

- New SpaceMouse feel: orbit and pan are now smooth — the ball sets a speed, the view eases in as you roll and glides to a stop after you let go, instead of jumping with the ball like a mouse. Set how long it glides (Speed & direction → Motion feel), or turn it off for direct control. Each program keeps its own.
- The label next to the pointer (Orbit, Pan…) is now off. Turn it back on in Buttons & keys → Extras if you want it.
