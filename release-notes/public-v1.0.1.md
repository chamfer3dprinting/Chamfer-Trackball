# Chamfer Trackball v1.0.1 · 2026-10-30

- Normal mouse pointing, clicks and scrolling remain the default.
- Optional Ball navigation: roll to orbit, tap Back to switch orbit/pan, and draw clockwise/anticlockwise circles to zoom in/out.
- Customize and save orbit, pan and zoom held keys, button combinations and per-program controls.
- Includes native Blender/Fusion adapters and editable mouse-emulation profiles for other modelling applications.
- About & updates installs newer published public versions and verifies the executable checksum before restarting.
- The M512's standard X/Y and wheel reports do not measure independent physical ball twist. Physical gesture feel and live CAD hosts still need owner testing.

Validation: 101 core checks, both app interfaces and release-policy checks passed. The public download is checked against the verified candidate checksum during publishing.
