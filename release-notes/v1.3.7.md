# Chamfer Trackball v1.3.7

2026-10-29

- Default stays a normal mouse: hold Ctrl and roll to rotate, hold Shift and roll to pan, Shift + wheel to zoom in / out.
- Top-right is now Twist mode: tap it and roll the ball left / right to twist (roll) the model around the view, like a 3D mouse cap. The wheel zooms in / out. Tap again or click for the mouse.
- Twist works in Chamfer3D Studio once its Preview with the trackball link is published; in other apps Twist mode rotates instead.
