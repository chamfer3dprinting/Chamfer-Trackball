# Chamfer Trackball v1.3.5

2026-10-29

- New 3D modelling layout (now the default): the ball is a normal mouse until you ask for 3D. Hold Ctrl and roll to rotate; hold Shift and roll to pan, Shift + wheel moves in / out.
- Rotate mode and Translate mode without holding anything: tap the top-left button for Rotate, the top-right button for Translate. Tap the same button again, or click, for the mouse.
- The wheel is the twist: in Rotate it rolls the model around the view (in Chamfer3D Studio), in Translate it moves in / out.
- True 3D in Chamfer3D Studio: rotation is free in every direction (like a 3D mouse) around the surface under your pointer, with roll. Needs the matching Chamfer3D Studio update; other apps use their normal orbit / pan / zoom.
- New quick setup: 3D controller (Buttons & keys).
