# Chamfer Trackball v1.4.3

2026-10-29

- New on-screen guide, like a 3D mouse: when you tap top-right (Rotate), top-left (Translate) or click back to the mouse, a picture shows which button does what, the mode name, and what rolling the ball and turning the wheel do (Roll / Twist or In / out).
- Pivot: while rotating, a dot shows the spot the model turns around — whatever you're pointing at when you start. In Chamfer3D Studio the model rotates around the surface under the pointer.
- Both can be switched off in Buttons & keys → Extras.
