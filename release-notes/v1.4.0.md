# Chamfer Trackball v1.4.0

2026-10-29

- Side (left) wheel cycles what the ball does in 3D modelling: Mouse → Rotate → Pan → Twist, one step per notch; turn it the other way to go back. A click still returns to the mouse.
- Or put something else on the side wheel (Buttons & keys): switch programs, or a keyboard shortcut for up and another for down, plus the old zoom / scroll choices.
- The Normal mouse program keeps the side wheel as a normal wheel.
