# Chamfer Trackball v1.2.1

2026-10-28

- The ball does it all: the top-left key just swaps Normal mouse and 3D modelling. In 3D modelling, roll the ball to orbit, draw small circles to zoom (clockwise in, anticlockwise out, like turning a dial), and give it a quick left-right shake to swap between orbit and pan. No other buttons, no keyboard.
- A small ↻ or ✥ flashes by the pointer when a shake swaps orbit and pan (no words). Turn it off in Buttons & keys → Extras.
- 3D orbit, 3D pan and 3D zoom from v1.2.0 become one "3D modelling" program, keeping their speeds and DPI.
