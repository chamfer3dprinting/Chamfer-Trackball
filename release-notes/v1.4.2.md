# Chamfer Trackball v1.4.2

2026-10-29

- Ctrl and Shift are normal again in 3D modelling: hold Ctrl (or Shift) and click edges to select several, and the ball just moves the pointer.
- Rotate (tap top-right) and Translate (tap top-left) are unchanged. Zoom with the wheel, or the wheel in Translate.
- Want a hold key back? Buttons & keys → Hold keys (pick one your app doesn't use for selecting).
