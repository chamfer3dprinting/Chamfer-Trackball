# Chamfer Trackball v1.0.2

2026-10-28

- The trackball picture now matches the Nulea M512: four buttons around the ball (Middle top-left, Back top-right, Left and Right at the bottom) and a wheel on each side. The Forward button is gone because the M512 doesn't have one.
- New "What it's doing" log on Home and Buttons & keys: shows every button you hold, which app it saw, and what it sent — or why it didn't react.
- Fixed the Try it cube not reacting after the window was moved.
- Fixed a possible freeze of the mouse listener while the Chamfer Trackball window was busy.
- Warns you if Windows blocks the movement (for example when the 3D app runs as administrator).
