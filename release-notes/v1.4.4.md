# Chamfer Trackball v1.4.4

2026-10-29

- Buttons can do something else while Rotate or Translate is on (like a 3D mouse): Buttons & keys → While Rotate or Translate is on. Choose Snap / fit view, a keyboard shortcut, a macro, a program, or the other mode.
- Default in 3D modelling: the right button snaps / fits the view while a mode is on (it's a normal right click otherwise). A left click still goes back to the mouse.
- The on-screen guide shows that button's command.
