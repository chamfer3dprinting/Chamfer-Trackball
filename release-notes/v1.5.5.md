# Chamfer Trackball v1.5.5

2026-10-29

- Combinations save as soon as you add them — even before you've ticked two buttons or picked a command. (A half-made one is kept but doesn't do anything until it has two buttons.)
- New: General → Reset everything. Puts every program, button, wheel, combination, speed and the guide back to normal settings; your macros and look are kept.
- Reset buttons & keys now resets this program to its own normal layout (3D modelling or Normal mouse), including its combinations.
