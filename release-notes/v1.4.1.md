# Chamfer Trackball v1.4.1

2026-10-29

- Tap top-right: Rotate on — just roll the ball to rotate, no Ctrl needed. Tap again (or click) and it's a normal mouse. A small ↻ Rotate on / ➚ Mouse sign shows by the pointer so you can see it switch.
- Hold Ctrl and roll: zoom in / out (roll up = in). Hold Shift and roll: pan.
- While Rotate is on, the wheel twists the model in Chamfer3D Studio (switch: Buttons & keys → Extras → Twist with the wheel; off = the wheel zooms).
- Tap top-left: Translate on — just roll to slide the view, the wheel moves in / out. Tap again (or click) for the mouse.
- The side wheel switches programs (Normal mouse ↔ 3D modelling).
