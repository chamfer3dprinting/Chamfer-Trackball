# Chamfer Trackball v1.1.1

2026-10-28

- 3D mode, trackball only — like a SpaceMouse: press one button and the ball becomes a 3D controller. Just roll to orbit, scroll to zoom, hold your pan button to pan, no keyboard needed. Clicking still works, the view lets go as soon as the ball stops, and pressing the button again (or Esc) gives you the normal pointer back. It turns itself off when you leave the 3D app.
- New quick setup "Trackball only (like a SpaceMouse)": Back = 3D mode on/off, hold Middle = pan, hold Right + roll or scroll = zoom.
- More precise movement: slow, small rolls now move the model less (down to about a third) while fast rolls stay full speed, so you can line up a view exactly. Turn it off on Speed & direction → Precision.
- Speed & direction warns when Windows "Enhance pointer precision" is on, because it makes the same roll turn the model by different amounts, and opens the setting for you.
