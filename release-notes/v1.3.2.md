# Chamfer Trackball v1.3.2

2026-10-29

- Hold the top-right button for half a second to go straight back to orbit (a quick tap still goes orbit → pan → zoom).
- Left and right click work in 3D modelling: the view is let go first, then your click goes through, so the app doesn't ignore it.
- A small nudge of the ball (like when you press a button) no longer starts moving the model.
- Esc is left alone for your app; Chamfer Trackball no longer uses it.
